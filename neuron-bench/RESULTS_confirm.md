Wiki p = two-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank on per-passage mean NLL differences (normal approximation with tie correction). Moved (stage-2 gate) = MMLU/ARC unadjusted McNemar p < 0.2; Wiki perplexity-ratio 95% CI excludes 1.

## Run notes

- Environment: results/confirm/ENV.txt (pip freeze identical to results/screen/ENV_orig.txt; torch 2.15.0+cpu, transformers 5.22.1; model snapshot 851bf6e806efd8d0a36b00ddf55e13ccb7b8cd0a). 4 vCPU Intel Xeon (AMX/AVX512-bf16), 15.6 GB RAM visible to the guest plus a VM memory balloon.
- bench.py's memory guard aborted the first stage-1 launch on MemAvailable (7.1 GB < 10.5 GB; the balloon hides free memory) with no other python process above 2 GB RSS, so stage 1 ran with --no-guard. The stock rerun and the stage-2 runs passed the guard (the balloon had deflated) and ran without --no-guard.
- Stage-1 process 1 (plain bench.py) ran stock, nisten_L8N1955_d-5 and the first 845 rows of nisten_L8N1955_d0. It was stopped (SIGTERM, 17:30 IST) because the balloon re-inflated and evicted the mmapped weights (~5 s/item instead of ~0.5 s). Process 2 resumed (bench.py's per-item resume; no duplicate rows, one config_hash per arm) through `run_anon.py`, which runs bench.py unchanged after copying the weights bit-exactly into anonymous memory. All later stage-1 arms and all stage-2 controls ran through run_anon.py. The stage-1 stock run and the stock rerun both ran as plain bench.py.
- Because process 2 re-listed stock and nisten_L8N1955_d-5 with 0 new rows, their summary.json files were restored to the process-1 versions. nisten_L8N1955_d0's summary covers only the 155 resumed rows.
- The VM was suspended once for about 15 minutes during nisten_L8N1955_d2 (kernel 'virtual machine fork' events); the guest clock did not advance, so its wall s in the table above (657 s) excludes the pause (wall-clock duration about 26 min). Results are unaffected.
- Calibration (calib200, layers 8, 13, 14) was computed once in stage-1 process 1 and cached in results/calib/; process 2 and the stage-2 controls reuse that cache.
- Readings of A3 where it leaves a choice: the Holm family mixes McNemar p (MMLU, ARC) and Wilcoxon p (Wiki); the stage-2 gate was checked for dose-0 cells too (none moved); 'damages' on Wiki = perplexity increase with Holm p < 0.05 and increase rank 1/21; 'perplexity rise below 5%' uses the point estimate of the ratio; bootstrap CIs are percentile intervals; Wilcoxon drops zero differences and uses the normal approximation with tie correction (matches scipy.stats.wilcoxon method='approx').
